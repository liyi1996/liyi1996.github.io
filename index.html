01 / ABOUT

A mathematician at the intersection of theory and computation.

My research centers on the construction and classification of permutation polynomials over finite fields. I am interested in how algebraic structure, finite geometry and combinatorics reveal new families of permutation functions and their properties.

Beyond finite-field problems, I study mathematical questions in coding theory and cryptography, including Boolean functions and nonlinear maps. I am exploring how AI can accelerate mathematical research, and how to understand mathematical reasoning by AI.
